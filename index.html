How it works

The phone decides before the photo leaves.

No cloud call in the seal path. Everything below runs on the device — this prototype runs it in your browser.

01 · CAPTURE

Live camera

Frame grabbed from the live stream. There is no gallery path into a seal.

02 · BODY

IMU + GNSS

Step/still check from the accelerometer, a GNSS fix, and the two must agree. Disagreement refuses.

03 · SEAL

SHA-256, local

Hash of frame bytes + sensor packet, computed on the phone.

04 · EDIT CHECK

Perceptual hash

Best-effort dHash compare of the after-photo. Never claims to catch every deepfake.

05 · DESK

Read-only review

Laptop verifies and accepts. It cannot mint a seal or override a refusal.

Guided demo · 30 seconds

Try it as a judge

  1. Open Phone (Seal). On a laptop it opens in Judge mode with a built-in Ward 14 scene.
  2. Tap Pass, Gallery, Spoof and Edit. Watch sensors tick and the checklist resolve to SEALED or REFUSED with the failed check named.
  3. Tap Send to desk (or download the .frameseal.json).
  4. Open Laptop (Review Desk). Accept the sealed card; refused cards stay blocked. Hit Verify, then Tamper a copy to watch the desk catch an edited card.
  5. Optional: switch to Live sensors on a phone — real rear camera, accelerometer step count and GPS. Walk two steps toward the subject, then seal.
Who it's for

The reviewer, not the worker

The person who has to sign off on a photo — pay a wage, close a ward ticket, release a delivery — today has nothing but a JPEG and trust. FrameSeal gives them a card that says how the frame was captured, and refuses when the phone's own sensors disagree.
