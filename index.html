How it works

The phone decides before the photo leaves.

No cloud call in the seal path. Every step runs on the device — this prototype runs it in your browser.

01 · CAPTURE

Live camera

Frame grabbed from the live stream. There is no gallery path into a seal.

02 · BODY

IMU + GNSS

Step / still check and a GNSS fix — and they must agree. Disagreement refuses.

03 · SEAL

SHA-256, local

Hash of frame bytes + sensor packet, computed on the phone.

04 · EDIT CHECK

Perceptual hash

Best-effort dHash compare of the after-photo. Never claims to catch every deepfake.

05 · DESK

Read-only review

Laptop verifies and accepts. It cannot mint a seal or override a refusal.

Guided demo · 30 seconds

Try it as a judge

  1. Open Phone (Seal). On a laptop it opens in Judge mode with a built-in Ward 14 scene.
  2. Tap Pass, Gallery, Spoof and Edit. Sensors tick and the checklist resolves to SEALED or REFUSED, naming the failed check.
  3. Tap Send to desk (or download the .frameseal.json).
  4. Open Laptop (Review Desk). Accept the sealed card; refused cards stay blocked. Hit Verify, then Tamper a copy to watch the desk catch an edited card.
  5. On a phone, switch to Live sensors — real rear camera, accelerometer steps and GPS. Walk two steps toward the subject, then seal.
Who it's for

The reviewer, not the worker

The person who pays a wage, closes a ward ticket, or releases a delivery has nothing but a JPEG and trust. FrameSeal hands them a card that says how the frame was captured.
